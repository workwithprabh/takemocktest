# Exam calendar backlink outreach

Asset to pitch: https://takemocktest.com/in/exam-calendar

Government-focused alternative: https://takemocktest.com/in/upcoming-government-exams

## Outreach rules

- Contact only organizations whose page already helps students with competitive exams, career guidance or learning resources.
- Ask for editorial consideration, never demand a link or offer payment.
- Mention the exact page where the calendar would help their students.
- Lead with official-source links, clear tentative-date labels and free calendar downloads.
- Send one personalized message, then one follow-up after 7 to 10 days. Stop after that.
- Recheck every target page before contacting it. A listing here is a prospect, not an endorsement or guaranteed placement.
